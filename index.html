About Me

Toronto, Ontario · Authorized to work in Canada

With a master’s in Computational Science and a bachelor’s in Computer Engineering, I bring technical understanding to design decisions. My background in AI and machine learning informs how I approach AI-enabled products, agentic workflows, and collaboration with engineers.
